Search by Colors, Clothes, and What Happened in the Photo

Fixes searches where words get mixed up • Best for: Photos taken over a year ago

4.43 / 5.00
Helpfulness to Users: 4.8 / 5.0 User Review Proof: 4.0 / 5.0 Ease of Building: 4.2 / 5.0 Ease of Using: 4.5 / 5.0

How it works: Connect colors and details directly to people and objects (like 'friend wearing yellow jacket') so search won't confuse clothes with insects (yellowjackets) or show the wrong photos. Also understand what was NOT in the photo (like 'not smiling' or 'no hat').

Connect Colors to Clothes

Teach search to pair words together correctly (for example, link "yellow" to "jacket"), so searching for a yellow jacket won't bring up bees or yellow cars.

Understand "Not" Clues

Help search understand when users remember something that was missing (like "not smiling" or "without glasses"), removing hundreds of wrong photos.

Helpful Suggestion Buttons

Show quick buttons under the search bar (like "Was it outdoors?" or "What color was the shirt?") so users don't have to guess the right words.

Expected Impact: 14% more people find their photos • 28% fewer people give up searching
